About

A little more about me

I'm a UX Designer with 4+ years designing digital experiences, owning work from research through production handoff across B2B SaaS, B2C and enterprise products on web and mobile. I started out in mechanical engineering before pivoting to design at IIT Kanpur — that background still shapes how I approach problems: system-first, grounded in how things actually work.

Today I own UX across four product lines within TCS's SBI banking engagement — spanning consumer-facing banking, internal operations and workforce tools — leading a team of visual designers and driving design strategy and roadmap prioritization for SBI YONO. I also bring AI and GenAI into everyday design work, using them for research synthesis, ideation and rapid prototyping to ship faster.
